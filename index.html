Why SongScript exists

Some songs don't just play.
They transport.

There's a song — you know the one. The first two seconds and you're back. Not just remembering, but actually there: the smell of the room, the feeling in your chest, the person sitting next to you.

Those memories deserve more than a playlist. They deserve words. SongScript uses AI to take everything you felt about that song and shape it into a polished, personal letter — written in your voice, beautiful enough to keep forever.

Your first one is free. No templates. No generic output. Just a letter that sounds like you — on your best writing day.
